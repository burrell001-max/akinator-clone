Genie Guessing Game: Think of Someone and the Genie Guesses

The genie guessing game is simple to start and hard to put down. You think of a character, the Genie asks questions, and you find out whether it can name your pick. MindGenie AI is free, needs no sign-up and works in any modern browser.

🔮 Play the genie guessing game

What a round looks like

  1. Pick in secret. Choose any real or fictional character and keep them in mind. Don't type or say the name.
  2. Answer the questions. The Genie asks things like "Is your character a real person?" You can answer Yes, No, Probably, Probably not or Not sure.
  3. Watch the confidence meter. It shows how sure the Genie is as the possibilities shrink.
  4. Get the guess. The Genie names a character. Tell it whether it was right, and if it wasn't, you can optionally tell it who you were thinking of.

You may know it by another name

People search for this kind of game in many ways: a genie guesser, a guessing genie, an online genie, a mind reading website, or even with spellings like "geenie", "ginie" and "jinny". They all describe the same idea, a character guessing game where a friendly genie works out who you are thinking of. MindGenie AI is an independent project and is not affiliated with Akinator or any other guessing game.

What the Genie can guess

The Genie knows characters from movies, TV shows, anime, video games and history. Well-known characters tend to be guessed quickly. Obscure ones, or characters who look and behave a lot like others, are harder. See how hard popular picks are in our guides to Batman, Goku and Sherlock Holmes.

Yes. There are no purchases, no downloads and no accounts. The site is supported by ads, and you can read exactly what we collect in the Privacy Policy.
