Character Guesser: Movies, TV, Anime, Games and History

MindGenie AI is a character guesser: you pick someone from a story or from history, and the Genie tries to work out who it is from a short series of questions. This page is a map of what you can pick from and how to choose a character that makes the game interesting.

🔮 Start guessing

Browse by category

  • Movie characters: superheroes, villains and franchise icons such as Darth Vader and Iron Man.
  • TV show characters: from Walter White to Michael Scott.
  • Anime characters: including Naruto Uzumaki and Levi Ackerman.
  • Video game characters: such as Mario, Kratos and Samus Aran.
  • Historical figures: like Cleopatra, Nikola Tesla and Joan of Arc.

What makes a character easy or hard to guess

The Genie does best with characters that have clear, widely agreed traits: a distinctive costume, a well-known role, a famous setting. It struggles more with characters whose traits are ambiguous or shared with many others, such as supporting characters, anti-heroes whose morality is debated, or people from crowded historical periods. Each character guide on this site rates how tricky that pick tends to be and lists the traits the Genie will probably ask about.

Picking a character that makes the game fun

  • Choose someone you know well enough to answer questions confidently.
  • If you want a quick win, pick an icon like Batman or Pikachu.
  • If you want a challenge, pick someone with a twist, such as a villain who looks like a hero.

Real or fictional, either works

You aren't limited to made-up characters. Scientists, rulers and explorers are all fair game. The only rule is that you have to be able to describe them with simple traits.
