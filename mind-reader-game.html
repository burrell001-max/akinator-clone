Mind Reader Game: Can the Genie Read Your Mind?

A good mind reader game feels a little like magic: you think of someone, answer a handful of simple questions, and a screen names the person you had in mind. MindGenie AI is a free online version of that idea. There is nothing to download and no account to create. You just open the page, pick a character in your head, and the Genie starts asking.

🔮 Start the mind reader game

It isn't telepathy, but it's close

The Genie can't actually see inside your head, of course. What it does is ask questions that split the possibilities in half as quickly as it can: is the character real, are they human, do they appear in a movie, do they wear a mask. Every answer removes a large group of characters from the running, and a confidence meter climbs as the field narrows. After roughly 10 to 20 questions it usually has enough to make a guess. If you want the full explanation, read how an AI guessing game works.

How to play

  • Choose anyone you know well: a movie hero, an anime rival, a game protagonist or a historical figure.
  • Answer each question with Yes, No, Probably, Probably not or Not sure. Honest answers work best.
  • When the Genie makes a guess, tell it whether it was right. That feedback is saved as an anonymous statistic.

Tips to beat the mind reader

If you want to stump the Genie, don't pick the most famous name that comes to mind. Pick someone with traits that overlap with other characters, such as a side character from a big franchise, or a historical figure from a crowded era. Our list of 50 characters that stump the Genie is a good place to look for ideas. If you'd rather see the Genie succeed, choose a character with very distinctive features and answer with confidence.

Works on phones and computers

The game runs in your browser and is built to be played on a phone as easily as on a desktop. The first round of the day can take a few extra seconds while the guessing engine wakes up; after that, rounds start straight away.
