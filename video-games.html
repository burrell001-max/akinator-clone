How to pick and answer for this category

Game characters are unusual because the player often controls them, so their personalities can be quieter than those of film or TV characters. A silent protagonist may still have strong traits in the Genie's data, such as a signature weapon or armor, while an AI such as GLaDOS or Cortana is mostly defined by a voice.

Pay attention to two questions in particular. "Is your character human?" has different answers for Kratos, Samus, Snake and the two AIs. "Is your character known to almost everyone?" is a Yes for a mascot like Pikachu and closer to Probably for most other game characters, since fame inside gaming is not the same as fame everywhere.

Choose a single game when a series has several entries. Kratos, for example, changes considerably between the Greek and Norse games.
