How to pick and answer for this category

Game characters are unusual because the player often controls them, so their personalities can be quieter than those of film or TV characters. A silent protagonist may still have strong traits in the Genie's data, such as a signature weapon or armor, while an AI such as GLaDOS or Cortana is mostly defined by a voice.

Pay attention to two questions in particular. "Is your character human?" has different answers for Kratos, Samus, Snake and the two AIs. "Is your character known to almost everyone?" is a Yes for a mascot like Pikachu and closer to Probably for most other game characters, since fame inside gaming is not the same as fame everywhere.

Choose a single game when a series has several entries. Kratos, for example, changes considerably between the Greek and Norse games.

For a games night, the guide to playing with friends covers passing one device around, scoring a session and settling arguments about which version of a character counts. If you want to know what a character's own page adds, see how to read a character guide.

Answering for a character you control

Game characters are strange subjects because the player is often the one deciding what they do. A silent protagonist may have almost no personality in the script and still have a very distinctive set of traits: a weapon, an armour, a role in a repeating story. When a question asks whether your character is a hero, answer for the role the game gives them rather than for the choices you made while playing.

The other trap is the series question. Characters who appear across many entries change more than film characters do, because each game is built to feel different. Picking one entry before you start — and answering only for that entry — prevents the contradictions that make a game character round stall.

Why artificial intelligences are awkward picks

Two of the characters in this category are not people at all, and one of them is not even physical. Questions about being human, wearing a costume and having a body all behave oddly for an AI, and a player who answers them by intuition rather than by the character's actual nature will send the round in the wrong direction. Answer literally: a program is not human, does not wear clothes, and does not have a job in the ordinary sense.

Frequently asked questions

How many game characters are in the roster?

Six at present, including a bounty hunter, a soldier, an AI and a mascot. The cards above list each one.

Is Pikachu counted as a game character?

He appears in both the games and the anime, and the roster files him under anime. Either category is a reasonable place to look.

Why is Samus hard to guess?

Because armoured science-fiction characters overlap heavily, so one uncertain answer about weapons or setting can move the round.

Should I answer for the player character or the story character?

For the character as the story presents them, not for the choices you made while playing.
