Maleficent: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Maleficent is the roster's clearest fairy-tale villain: fictional, magical, a villain by the engine's own answer, royal, and the title character of her own film. The partials come from her two versions, which disagree about how human and how animated she is.

Dark fairy, later the title character of her own film
Known for

Why the round is short

Villainy is the strongest answer she has. The roster holds few of them, and fewer still who are also magical, royal and famous, so the round collapses quickly once that question is answered yes.

The partials are the animated and human questions. The 1959 character is animated and a fairy; the 2014 character is live-action and more human in framing, so the engine scores both in between.

Who she is

The fairy who curses Aurora to prick her finger and die, then turns into a dragon when the plan is interrupted. She is one of Disney's most stylised villains, and the 2014 film rebuilds her as the wronged protagonist.

That second version is why the engine's answers wobble: the same name now covers a villain and a heroine.

Where players get tripped up

The powers question. She is magical, but the engine stores a yes on powers without storing a weapon, so answering yes to both points at Thanos and Vader.

The costume question is a no. Horns and robes are a look, not a disguise, and the engine treats them that way.

Animated grandeur, live-action revision

Sleeping Beauty was designed in a flat, decorative style that made Maleficent's silhouette unmistakable, and her dragon transformation is still one of the best pieces of hand-drawn animation in the Disney catalogue.

The 2014 film rebuilds her as the wronged party, which is a different character with the same name. The engine stores a partial on humanity and animation precisely because both versions exist.
