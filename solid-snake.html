Solid Snake: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Solid Snake is the infiltration specialist of the Metal Gear series and one of the harder picks in the video game category. He is not difficult because he is obscure. He is difficult because the answers that describe him also describe a bounty hunter in power armour, a Spartan soldier and a hacker from a science-fiction film.

Role

Known for
Infiltrating armed facilities alone
Powers
None. Training, equipment and stealth
Appears in
Metal Gear and Metal Gear Solid games

Why the Genie takes the long way round

Snake answers yes to several questions that are individually common but overlap heavily with other characters: human, no powers, military, armed, set in the present or near future, and dressed for combat. Every one of those answers removes some characters and leaves a cluster of soldiers, bounty hunters and armoured leads behind.

The round usually narrows to a group in which Snake, Samus Aran and Kratos are all still possible. Only once the questions turn to setting and equipment does the field separate, and by then a round can be most of the way to its limit.

Who he is

Snake is a clone of the soldier known as Big Boss, created for a government project and then used by the same institutions that made him. He works alone, usually by infiltration rather than assault, and his most famous missions involve walking into a facility that is already on alert and coming out with something the people inside did not want him to have.

He is a soldier rather than a superhero. His advantages are preparation, patience and a talent for not being seen, not strength or flight.

Traits that give him away

  • Human: Yes. He is a human being, engineered rather than superhuman.
  • Powers: No. Everything he does comes from training and equipment.
  • Soldier: Yes. Special forces is the core of the character.
  • Uses weapons: Yes. Firearms, and hand-to-hand techniques of his own.
  • Costume or suit: Partly. A sneaking suit is gear rather than a disguise, and the engine scores it in between.
  • Modern setting: Yes, near-future military rather than fantasy.

How a round usually runs

Expect the early questions to establish that the character is human, powerless and military. The middle of the round is where Snake becomes expensive: questions about armour, weapons and science fiction keep several characters alive at once.

The questions that finally settle him are about role and setting. A soldier who works alone in a modern facility is a narrow description, and once the round reaches it the answer arrives quickly.

Where players get tripped up

The common mistake is answering yes to powers, because Snake survives things an ordinary person would not. That answer sends the round towards super-soldiers and away from him.

The second mistake is mixing games. Snake ages visibly across the series, and a question about age or era can be answered two ways depending on which entry you have in mind. Pick one entry before the first question and answer only for it.

Tips for a round with Snake

  • Answer no on powers.: He is exceptional, not superhuman.
  • Answer yes on soldier.: That is the most useful single fact about him.
  • Commit to one game.: His age and equipment change between entries.
  • Expect the round to run long.: That is the character, not a mistake.

When to pick Solid Snake

Pick Snake when you want a hard round that still feels fair. He is famous enough that most players can answer for him, and his traits are specific enough that a careful round will still reach him. If you want to watch the engine work through a crowded part of its roster, he is a better demonstration than any character who is guessed in six questions.
