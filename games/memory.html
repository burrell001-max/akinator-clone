Memory Match

Twelve cards, six pairs, and a move counter that does not lie.

Moves: 0 Pairs: 0 / 6 00:00

How to play

Every card hides one of six symbols, and each symbol appears exactly twice. Turn over two cards per move. If they match, they stay face up; if they do not, they turn back after a moment and the move is counted. Clear the board and the game is over.

There is no countdown. The timer starts on your first flip and stops when the last pair is found, so a slow, careful game costs you nothing but time you chose to spend.

Playing it well

Matching is mostly a memory exercise, but the last few moves are not. Once six cards remain and you know where three pairs are, the order you flip them in does not matter — you cannot lose. The moves that decide your score are the early ones, where a card you have already seen is worth more than a card you have not.

The counter is honest: every pair of flips is one move, including a pair you already know matches. A perfect game with no wasted flips is impossible unless you get lucky, but ten to twelve moves is a good run.

What gets saved

Your best move count is stored in your browser's local storage under a single key, so it survives a refresh and stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no name attached to it, and clearing your browser data clears the record too.
