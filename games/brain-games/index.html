About this category

These are the games you can start without reading a manual and still lose to on the first try. Most ask for the same three habits: scan the board before acting, keep a note of what you have ruled out, and stop when the next move is a guess rather than an inference.

What they train

Deduction, working memory and the discipline of checking a move before committing to it. Several of these games (Codebreaker, Crossword Detective, Shift Sort) are pure logic: every position has exactly one correct next step, and the score is how quickly you find it rather than how fast you can click.
