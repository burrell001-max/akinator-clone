About this category

All five are the same experiment in different clothing. Something is shown, it is taken away, and you reproduce it. What changes is the material — positions, sounds, digits, shapes — and how much of it arrives at once.

What they train

Short-term and working memory, with a specific emphasis on chunking: grouping items so a sequence of twelve becomes four groups of three rather than twelve separate things to hold.

How to play

Every game opens straight into its board. Tap, click or use the arrow keys; each card above explains the goal in one line.

  • Memory Match — Flip cards two at a time and match all six pairs.
  • Pattern Echo — Repeat the sequence of pads as it grows by one each round.
  • Grid Flash — Click the squares that lit up before they fade from memory.
  • Digit Echo — Type back the string of digits that flashed on screen.
  • Order Recall — Click the shapes back in the order they were numbered.
No setup required. Progress is stored in your own browser under mg- keys — clearing site data resets it.
