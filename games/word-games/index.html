About this category

The word games here use a modest, everyday vocabulary on purpose. A puzzle you lose because the answer was an archaic plural teaches nothing; a puzzle you lose because you missed a common word teaches you to look again.

What they train

Vocabulary recall, anagram fluency and — in Word Guess — the same elimination logic as the number and codebreaking games, applied to letters instead of digits.

How to play

Every game opens straight into its board. Tap, click or use the arrow keys; each card above explains the goal in one line.

  • Anagram Hunt — Unscramble the letters of the word the clue describes.
  • Word Guess — Find the hidden five-letter word in six guesses.
  • Mini Crossword — Fill the word square so every across and down answer fits.
  • Clue Cards — Answer ten crossword clues using their enumerations.
  • Clue Anagrams — Rearrange the shuffled letters to match each clue.
  • Daily Clues — Answer five daily clues with one attempt each.
  • Crossword Detective — Find the letters that were changed in a finished word square.
