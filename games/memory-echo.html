Pattern Echo

Four pads, one growing sequence, one mistake allowed.

Step: 0 Your best: —

How to play

Press start and one pad lights up. Repeat it by pressing the same pad. The next round adds one more step to the end of the sequence, so by the tenth round you are remembering eleven presses in order. A wrong pad ends the run.

You can use the keyboard instead: the keys 1, 2, 3 and 4 map to the pads in reading order, top-left to bottom-right. The replay button repeats the current sequence without penalty, once the game has started and it is your turn.

Rules and scoring

  • The sequence starts at one step and grows by one after each success.
  • Progress is measured in steps: the highest sequence length you have cleared is the score.
  • Nothing is timed. Take as long as you like before pressing a pad.
  • Your best step count is stored in your browser under one mg- key and never leaves the device.

Where it sits on the shelf

The echo pad trains ordered recall: you have to reproduce a sequence, not recognise an item. That is a different job from Memory Match, where the same six symbols are on the table and the skill is remembering positions, and from Digit Echo, which drops the spatial element and asks you to type a string back. Grid Flash sits between the two: its squares have positions but no order.

Ten minutes spread across all four is a better session than ten minutes on one, because each asks for the same underlying memory in a form the others never practise.

Remembering longer sequences

Repeating the pad positions in order is the obvious method and it stops working around eight or nine steps, because the sequence is too long to hold as separate items. Two tricks help. Group the steps in threes as they play — three green, three blue — and hold six chunks rather than eighteen items. Then say the direction out loud or in your head: up-left, up-left, down-right. Words survive interference better than positions do.

The other trick is to stop translating. Once the mapping from pad to key is automatic, pressing 3 costs nothing to think about, which leaves more attention for remembering what comes next. If you are finding the pads hard to tell apart, the keyboard is not cheating; it is a cleaner channel.
