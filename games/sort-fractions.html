How to Play

Every card holds a number between zero and two, but they are not all written as fractions: some are decimals, some percentages, some mixed numbers like 1 1/4. Work out what each card is worth, then click the cards one at a time in ascending order of value.

A click is accepted only if the card is larger than the last one you placed. Get it wrong and the card shakes, your mistake count rises, and nothing moves. When all eight cards are placed the ladder is finished and your time is recorded.

Rules and scoring

  • Eight cards per ladder, each with a different value.
  • Order is strict: equal values are never generated, so there is always exactly one correct sequence.
  • Your best time for today’s ladder is kept in this browser under mg-ladder-best.
  • Finishing today’s ladder once a day keeps your streak alive; the streak is stored under mg-ladder-streak.
  • Practice sets are freshly generated and do not affect the streak.
  • The timer starts on your first click, so you can read the cards before the clock moves.

Comparing values quickly

The safest method is to convert everything to the same form, and the easiest common form here is a decimal. Half is 0.5, a fifth is 0.2, and an eighth is 0.125 — three conversions that cover most of the pool. A percentage is simply that decimal multiplied by a hundred, so 35% is 0.35 and 12.5% is 0.125. A mixed number is its whole part plus the decimal of the fraction: 1 3/8 is 1.375.

Two shortcuts save the arithmetic. First, group by whole part: everything below 1 comes before everything above it, so a single card above 1 is instantly last among small values. Second, compare fractions that share a denominator by looking only at the top number — 5/8 is bigger than 3/8 without any conversion at all. Where denominators differ but the fractions are close, cross-multiply: 3/5 against 5/8 becomes 24 against 25, so 5/8 wins.
