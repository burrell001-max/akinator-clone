About this category

A crossword is two puzzles at once. The clue is a definition with the grammar filed off, and the grid is a constraint system where every answer has to agree with the ones crossing it. The games here separate those halves so you can practise either one.

What they train

Lateral reading of definitions, vocabulary under time pressure, and the constraint satisfaction of a crossed grid — the same skill that makes a full-size cryptic tractable once you stop reading clues in isolation.

How to play

Every game opens straight into its board. Tap, click or use the arrow keys; each card above explains the goal in one line.

  • Mini Crossword — Fill the word square so every across and down answer fits.
  • Clue Cards — Answer ten crossword clues using their enumerations.
  • Clue Anagrams — Rearrange the shuffled letters to match each clue.
  • Daily Clues — Answer five daily clues with one attempt each.
  • Crossword Detective — Find the letters that were changed in a finished word square.
No setup required. Progress is stored in your own browser under mg- keys — clearing site data resets it.
