How the categories fit together

Ten categories, twelve games, and a few games wearing two labels. Sudoku is a number game as well as a sudoku; the mini crossword counts as a word game; Merge Up is filed under both number and puzzle. The rail counts follow the labels, so adding the numbers gives more than twelve.

Sort the shelf by category and the overlaps become visible: everything grouped under Words, then everything grouped under Numbers. Filtering is the faster route when you already know what kind of thinking you want.

A games room, not an arcade

Every page here is hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no webfont, no analytics script, no third-party ad tag, and no build step — the file you load is the file that runs. One game never loads another game's code.

That is also why the pages are quiet. There is no autoplay, no interstitial and no timer counting down at you unless the game itself is a timed one.

What is stored, and where

Best scores, streaks and solved states live in your browser's local storage under keys beginning mg-. They are never sent anywhere, no name is attached to them, and clearing site data removes them for good.

Daily games keep the dates they have seen and a streak count. Nothing else is written.
