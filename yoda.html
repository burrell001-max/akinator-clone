Yoda: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Yoda is one of the easiest characters in the roster to describe and one of the oddest to answer questions about. He is old, small, powerful and famous, and most of his stored answers are clean yeses. The difficulty is a handful of partials that decide whether the round ends with him or with another mentor.

Quick facts

Role

Powers
The Force: telekinesis, foresight, a lightsaber

Why the round is short

Yoda combines answers almost nobody else shares: fictional, powerful, a mentor, part of an order, from a fantasy setting, armed with a lightsaber. The engine picks whichever question best splits the remaining candidates, so a character far from the pack on several axes gets found quickly.

The ambiguous part is species. He is not human, so that answer removes every human character at once, and the remaining questions mostly confirm what the round already knows.

Who he is

A Jedi Master of an unnamed species who has trained Jedi for centuries, including Obi-Wan Kenobi and, briefly, Luke Skywalker. The original trilogy finds him in exile on Dagobah, where he dies after telling Luke he has learned what he needed to.

His role is nearly always the same: the old teacher who refuses a straight answer until the student is ready to hear one.

Where players get tripped up

The job question. Yoda holds no paid job in the ordinary sense, and the engine stores a no on the workplace question. Answering yes because he teaches pulls the round towards school settings.

The animation and book questions are both no. Answering yes because he appears in cartoons points the engine at characters like SpongeBob and BoJack.

Why the Genie might guess Thanos instead

Closest match on the sheet: Thanos, at an average distance of 0.18 across 28 questions.

They differ on 7 questions: hero yes against no; villain no against yes; famous companion partly against no; mind over strength yes against partly; fantasy world yes against partly; school or academy partly against no; mentor figure yes against no. Answer those differences deliberately and the round resolves; answer by habit and the guess is a coin toss between the two.

The puppet and the performance

The original Yoda was a puppet operated by Frank Oz, with a voice that has not changed in forty years. The prequels replaced him with a digital model and fans noticed. Neither version changes the stored answers, but it does explain why some players remember him as a practical effect and some as a cartoon.

His most quoted line is also the most useful advice for this game: there is no try, only a yes, a no, or an honest probably. The engine scores the middle answer as information rather than a refusal.
