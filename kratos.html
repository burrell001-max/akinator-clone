⚔️ Kratos

Kratos is a hardened warrior protagonist who began his story fighting gods of Greek mythology before later games shifted his setting to Norse mythology. His arc from vengeful antihero to more reflective father figure is one of gaming's most-cited character transformations.

Why the Genie takes a bit longer with this one
