🍥 Naruto Uzumaki

Naruto Uzumaki is the title character of one of the best-selling manga and anime franchises of all time — an orphaned ninja who dreams of becoming his village's leader while carrying a powerful spirit sealed inside him. His orange jumpsuit and whisker-marked face are instantly recognizable even to non-anime fans.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Naruto's specific combination of traits — ninja setting, a sealed spirit, a signature catchphrase-driven personality, and status as a franchise's title character — makes him one of the fastest guesses in the anime category. Only a couple of questions about setting and powers are usually needed.

Background on Naruto Uzumaki

Naruto Uzumaki is the protagonist of Naruto, a manga by Masashi Kishimoto that began serialization in Weekly Shonen Jump in 1999 and was adapted into a long-running anime. He is a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who is shunned by many villagers because the powerful Nine-Tailed Fox was sealed inside him when he was a baby.

Naruto's central goal is to become Hokage, the leader of his village, and gain the respect he has been denied. Along the way he forms bonds with his teammates Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno and his teacher Kakashi Hatake, masters techniques such as the Shadow Clone Jutsu and the Rasengan, and slowly changes from a loud outcast into a respected leader.

Traits that give them away

  • Ninja academy and teams. The story begins at a ninja academy and centers on a three-person team, so school and group questions are yes.
  • Leadership goal. His ambition to become Hokage touches on the leadership question.
  • Sealed beast. The Nine-Tailed Fox gives him extra power and shapes his entire story.
  • A rival. Sasuke is both a friend and a rival, a central relationship in the series.
  • Anime character. He is from a manga and anime, so the cartoon question is a yes.

Easy to confuse with

Naruto is most often confused with Goku and Luffy because all three are energetic shonen protagonists. The simplest separator is the origin: Goku is an alien, Luffy is a pirate, and Naruto is a ninja. Sasuke Uchiha is another frequent mix-up, since he appears in every major scene alongside Naruto, but he is a rival and an antihero rather than the title protagonist.

Tips for playing as Naruto Uzumaki

Naruto changes a lot between the early series and Shippuden, so decide in advance which version you are thinking about. For a more difficult round, think of one of the supporting ninja, such as Kakashi or Shikamaru, and compare how many answers change.
