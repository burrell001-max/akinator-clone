Who he is

A horse who starred in a nineties family sitcom and has spent the decades since being asked about it. The show uses him to examine addiction, regret and the difference between being liked and being good.

The engine records a job because he is an actor, and records a companion because Todd exists, even though BoJack is alone for most of the series in every way that matters.

Where players get tripped up

The hero question. He is the protagonist and not a hero, and the engine stores a no; answering yes points at animated leads like Shrek.

A comedy that turns into a reckoning

The show starts as Hollywood satire with animal puns and becomes an examination of addiction and apology over six seasons. Will Arnett voices BoJack with a sitcom cadence that makes the darker scenes land harder.

The ending refuses to resolve him, which is why the engine stores a partial on transformation. Players who expect a redemption arc answer yes; the show is careful not to give one.
