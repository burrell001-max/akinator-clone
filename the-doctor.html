Why the round takes a while

The answers are strong but numerous. Alien origin, science, a famous companion and an enemy are each big splits, so the engine burns through candidates quickly.

What slows it down is the human question, stored as a partial because the Doctor looks human, and the fantasy question, stored as a partial because the show mixes science fiction with myth.

Who they are

A Time Lord from Gallifrey who stole a TARDIS and has been travelling ever since, usually with one companion and usually into trouble. The character has been played by many actors, and regeneration is the in-story reason.

The engine records a transformation because regeneration is a change of body rather than of person, which is exactly the kind of ambiguity this roster struggles to represent.

Where players get tripped up

The weapon question is a no. The sonic screwdriver is a tool, and players who answer yes point the round at soldiers and Jedi.

The human question. A flat no sounds right, but the show insists the Doctor passes as human, and the engine stores the ambiguity.

One character, many faces

The Doctor has been played by more than a dozen actors, and regeneration turns recasting into plot. Each era has its own tone, which is why two players can describe the same character completely differently.

The engine stores the constants: alien, clever, armed with a tool rather than a weapon, travelling with a companion. Everything else depends on which Doctor the player grew up with.
