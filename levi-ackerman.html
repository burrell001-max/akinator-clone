⚔️ Levi Ackerman

By Kent B Williams · Published 1 November 2026

Levi Ackerman is a stern, exceptionally skilled soldier from a dark fantasy series about humanity's fight against giant humanoid creatures. Known for a clipped, no-nonsense personality and a fanatical devotion to cleanliness, he's become one of the most popular characters in the franchise despite a relatively small role in the overall story.

Why the Genie takes a bit longer with this one

Levi's traits — elite soldier, dark fantasy setting, supporting rather than title role — overlap with several other stoic side characters across anime, so the Genie usually needs a few extra questions distinguishing rank, setting details, and personality quirks before it converges.

Background on Levi Ackerman

Levi Ackerman is a major character in Attack on Titan, the manga by Hajime Isayama that was adapted into an acclaimed anime. He is a captain in the Survey Corps, the branch of the military that ventures outside the walls protecting humanity from the giant Titans, and is widely called the strongest soldier alive.

Levi fights using vertical maneuvering equipment, a gear system that lets soldiers swing through the air, and a pair of blades. He is known for his short stature, his serious personality, and an obsession with cleanliness. Behind his cold manner, he is deeply loyal to his comrades, particularly his commander, Erwin Smith.

Traits that give them away

  • Soldier and captain. He holds military rank in a defined organization, so job and group questions are a yes.
  • Blades. His twin blades are his signature tools.
  • No superpowers. His strength comes from skill and an Ackerman bloodline, not magic.
  • A grim setting. The world is under constant threat, which sets the tone for the whole series.
  • Supporting figure. Levi is a fan favorite but not the title protagonist, so the sole-protagonist question is a no.

How a round usually runs

Levi's round usually hinges on his role rather than his abilities. He is human, has no powers, uses bladed gear to fight enormous enemies, and holds a senior position in a military unit, and the engine needs several of those at once before it commits. Rounds involving him tend to run the full length of the question set.

Easy to confuse with

Levi is most easily confused with other Attack on Titan characters such as Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, and Erwin Smith. Eren is the protagonist and a Titan shifter. Mikasa shares Levi's Ackerman strength but is a different age and role. Outside the series, he is sometimes mixed up with other stoic, blade-wielding anime characters such as Kakashi or Zoro. The sole-protagonist question is one of the best separators.

Frequently asked questions

No. He is a human soldier whose skill comes from training and experience.

Which series is he from?

He appears in Attack on Titan, in both the manga and the anime.
