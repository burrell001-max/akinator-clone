Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit mindgenieai.xyz, what the game stores, and what choices you have. It covers the site itself and the third-party services the site uses. It does not cover other websites you reach by following a link.

The site is run by Kent B Williams, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the information described on this page. Questions about this policy, and requests about your data, go to hello@mindgenieai.xyz.

What the game does not need

There is no account, no sign-up and no login. You are never asked for your name, email address, date of birth or payment details to play. The character you are thinking of is not transmitted to us unless you choose to type it in after a wrong guess, and even then it is stored without anything that identifies you.

What is stored on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, for three things:

  • Language preference — the interface language you last selected, so the page opens in the same language next time.
  • Round history — the outcomes of your recent rounds, kept in your browser so the game can show your own history. It holds a maximum of fifty entries and never leaves your device.
  • A local view counter — a number incremented in your browser if the visitor counter cannot be fetched.

You can clear all of it at any time through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing it does not affect your ability to play.

What the game's server records

Rounds are run by a server so the engine can pick the next question. While a round is in progress the server holds a random session ID, your answers so far and which questions have already been asked. Sessions expire after two hours and are held in memory; they are not linked to a name, email address or account.

When a round ends, you can rate the guess. A rating stores the guessed character, whether it was right, and the random session ID. If you typed the character you had in mind, that name is stored too. These records are anonymous in the sense that there is no identifier that leads back to a person, and they are used only to review which characters are confused with each other.

Our hosting providers keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, timestamp and requested page, for security and troubleshooting. These are kept by the providers under their own retention rules.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

Advertising on this site is served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to enable them to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Google's own explanation of how it uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising cookies are set by Google, not by this site. The cookie policy lists what they are used for and how to remove them.

Google Analytics

The home page uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are opened. It records page views and general information such as device type, browser and approximate location derived from the request. It is not used to identify individual visitors, and the site does not send it the text you type into any field.

GoatCounter is also used for a simple, cookie-free page view count. It records the page requested and a coarse referrer; it does not set cookies and does not build a profile of you.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent message before advertising or analytics cookies are used. Advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage are all set to denied by default in those regions, and are only granted if you accept through that message. If you decline, you can still play; the site simply serves non-personalised advertising or no advertising cookies at all.

Advertising choices in the United States

Visitors in states with advertising privacy laws can manage their advertising privacy choices through Google's privacy message, which appears on the site and links to Google's ad settings. The same Google Ads Settings page works for anyone who wants to turn off personalised ads.

Children

The game is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from children, and there is nothing to sign up for. If you believe a child has sent personal information by email, contact us and the message will be deleted.

Asking for deletion

Because rounds are stored under a random session ID with no contact details, there is normally no record that can be tied to you. If you sent an email and want it deleted, write to hello@mindgenieai.xyz from the same address and ask. If you have a session ID from a specific round and want its rating record removed, include it and the character involved; that is enough to find the row. Requests are handled within 30 days.

To remove what is stored in your browser, clear site data for mindgenieai.xyz in your browser settings. For advertising and analytics cookies set by Google, use the links in the cookie policy or your browser's cookie controls.

Changes to this policy

If the services the site uses change, this page changes with them, and the date at the top is updated. Substantial changes are noted in the section they affect rather than silently rewritten.
