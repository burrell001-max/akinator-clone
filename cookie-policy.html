Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store. Some are needed for a page to work, some measure traffic, and some are used to choose which advertisement you see. This page explains which of those are in play on mindgenieai.xyz and how to control them.

The site is run by Kent B Williams, who is responsible for the cookies and similar technologies described here. Questions about this page go to hello@mindgenieai.xyz.

Cookies set by this site

None. The game does not set its own cookies. The preferences it remembers — interface language, recent round history and a fallback page-view count — are stored in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is not sent with every request. Local storage stays on your device until you clear it.

Cookies set by Google advertising

Advertisements on this site are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies for advertising purposes, including cookies that record which ads you have already seen and cookies used to make advertising more relevant to you. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites.

Google's own description of these cookies and how to manage them is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can turn off personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' advertising cookies at aboutads.info. Information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies set by Google Analytics

The home page uses Google Analytics to count visits. It sets first-party cookies with names beginning _ga that distinguish one browser from another so repeat visits are not counted as new people. These cookies do not contain your name or email address, and the site does not send the text you type into the game to Analytics. Google's analytics opt-out browser add-on is available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

GoatCounter, the visitor counter, does not set cookies at all. It counts page requests without storing anything in your browser.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent message appears before advertising or analytics cookies are used. Until you answer it, advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage are all set to denied, and Google is told so through consent mode. If you accept, the relevant cookies may be set. If you decline, the game still works.

Your choice can be changed at any time by clearing the consent state: open your browser's site-data settings for mindgenieai.xyz, delete the stored data, and reload the page, and the message will appear again.

Advertising choices in the United States

Visitors in US states with advertising privacy laws can use Google's privacy message to manage their advertising choices. The Google Ads Settings page is available to everyone and controls personalised advertising across sites that use Google's advertising services.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies. The exact wording differs, but the setting is usually under Privacy or Site data:

  • Chrome — Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Edge — Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.
  • Firefox — Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari — Settings or Preferences, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data.

Blocking all cookies does not stop you playing. It does mean you will see the same ads repeatedly and the site will not remember your language choice between visits.

Contact

If you think a cookie has been set on this site that is not described here, write to hello@mindgenieai.xyz with the page address and the cookie name, and it will be investigated.
