How to pick and answer for this category

Film characters work best when they have a clear visual identity and a few unmistakable traits, such as a costume, a signature weapon or a famous enemy. Those are exactly the things the Genie's questions are built to uncover.

Three habits make the biggest difference. First, decide which version you mean: Batman, Maleficent and Thanos all differ between films, comics and cartoons, and the Genie's data reflects only one picture of each. Second, use "Probably" for honest in-between cases, such as whether a character is a hero or a villain, because a middle answer is scored as a partial match. Third, watch the lookalikes printed on each card: the Genie often reaches those characters first, and the question that separates them is usually about role, setting or motive.

If you want a challenge, try the characters tagged Hard. They sit very close to another character, which means one careless answer can send the Genie to the wrong one.
