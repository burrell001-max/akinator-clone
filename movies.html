🎬 Movie Characters

Movies are the single most common category players think of — heroes, villains, sidekicks, and everything in between. Below are ideas grouped by how easy or hard they tend to be for the Genie, so you can pick your own difficulty.

Easy — the Genie usually nails these fast

Iconic, widely recognized characters with distinctive traits. Expect a correct guess in well under 15 questions.

Batman Darth Vader Harry Potter Shrek Iron Man Elsa The Joker Forrest Gump James Bond Indiana Jones

Medium — takes a bit more work

Well-known, but with traits that overlap other characters in the same franchise or genre.

Han Solo Katniss Everdeen Tony Montana Ellen Ripley The Terminator Jack Sparrow Neo Michael Corleone Wednesday Addams John Wick

Hard — genuine challenges

Sequel-only characters, ensemble side roles, or characters who share a lot of traits with someone more famous.

A background Stormtrooper A minor Bond henchman A one-scene cameo character A remake-only version of a classic role A character cut from the theatrical release An ensemble heist-crew member A voice-only animated side character
