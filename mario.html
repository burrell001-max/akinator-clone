🍄 Mario

Mario is the mustachioed plumber-turned-hero at the center of one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time. Decades of platformers, spin-offs, and appearances across nearly every gaming genre have made him arguably the most recognized video game character in the world.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Mario's status as a franchise mascot, distinctive visual design, and non-violent, family-friendly tone make him one of the fastest guesses in the video game category — the Genie typically converges after confirming video-game origin and a family-friendly tone.

Background on Mario

Mario is Nintendo's mascot and one of the best-known characters in the world. He was created by Shigeru Miyamoto and first appeared in the 1981 arcade game Donkey Kong, where he was called Jumpman. He became a star with Super Mario Bros. in 1985, and he has since appeared in platformers, kart racers, sports games, and role-playing games.

He is a plumber from Brooklyn who finds himself in the Mushroom Kingdom, where he regularly rescues Princess Peach from the villain Bowser. He is recognizable by his red cap, blue overalls, and mustache, and often travels with his brother Luigi and friends like Toad and Yoshi.

Traits that give them away

  • A regular job. He is a plumber, which makes the paying job question a yes.
  • Rescue quests. Many games involve saving a princess, which touches on royalty and romance questions.
  • A famous nemesis. Bowser is his long-running adversary.
  • Not superpowered. His power-ups, such as mushrooms and fire flowers, are items rather than innate abilities.
  • Highly famous. He is recognized almost everywhere, so the fame question is a yes.

Easy to confuse with

Mario is most often confused with his brother Luigi, who shares the look but is taller, in green, and more timid, and with Wario, his greedy rival. Sonic the Hedgehog is another long-running mascot but is an animal, not a human. The job question is a good separator, since Mario and Luigi are both plumbers, while the nemesis and sole-protagonist questions help distinguish the brothers.

Tips for playing as Mario

Mario is a strong choice for a quick win. If you want a harder round, pick a lesser-known character from his world such as Waluigi or Toadette. Remember that questions about superpowers are best answered as 'sometimes' if you think of power-ups, and as 'no' if you think of the character himself.
