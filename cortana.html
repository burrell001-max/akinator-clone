Cortana: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Cortana is an artificial intelligence with powers, science, heroism, an enemy and a partial team, and the engine files her as a hero where it files GLaDOS as a villain. That one stored answer is the whole difference between two otherwise similar sheets.

Quick facts

First appearance
Halo: Combat Evolved (2001)
Created by
Bungie
Role
UNSC AI, Master Chief's companion
Known for
The blue hologram and the countdown to rampancy
Powers
Hacking, data control and ship systems

Why the round is short

Science plus powers plus a game origin is a narrow set, and the hero answer removes the villainous AIs. The round usually reaches her within a handful of questions.

The partials are team and fame. She works with the UNSC without being a member in the ordinary sense, and she is famous among players rather than to everyone.

Who she is

A smart AI built from a cloned brain, assigned to the Master Chief and, over the course of the series, to the survival of humanity. She is the voice in his helmet and the closest thing the games have to a co-protagonist.

Her arc is about lifespan: smart AIs decay, and the later games turn that into the main story, which is why the engine stores a partial on transformation.

Where players get tripped up

The human question. She is modelled on a human and voiced by one, so yes is tempting, but she is software and the engine stores a no.

The weapon question is a no. She enables weapons rather than carrying one, and a yes points at soldiers.

Why the Genie might guess GLaDOS instead

Closest match on the sheet: GLaDOS, at an average distance of 0.16 across 28 questions.

They differ on 6 questions: hero yes against no; villain no against yes; team or organisation partly against no; royalty or leadership no against yes; nemesis partly against yes; major transformation partly against no. Answer those differences deliberately and the round resolves; answer by habit and the guess is a coin toss between the two.

An AI with an expiry date

Cortana is voiced by Jen Taylor and written as a partner rather than a tool, which is unusual for a shooter of its era. The later games make her decay a plot point, and the engine records that as a partial on transformation.

Microsoft also used the name for its assistant, which is why some players answer the fame question differently. In this roster she is the Halo character, and the stored answers follow the games.
