❄️ Elsa

Elsa is the Snow Queen of Arendelle in Disney's animated film Frozen. She was born with the power to create ice and snow, and her struggle to control it, and to reconnect with her sister Anna, is the heart of the story.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Elsa is an easy pick because her traits are so distinctive: she is an animated queen with magical ice powers in a fantasy kingdom. A few questions about royalty, magic, and animation usually narrow it down fast.

Background on Elsa

Frozen was released by Walt Disney Animation Studios in 2013 and became one of the highest-grossing animated films of its time. It is loosely inspired by Hans Christian Andersen's fairy tale The Snow Queen. Elsa, voiced by Idina Menzel, hides her powers out of fear after an accident harms her sister, then flees to the mountains and builds an ice palace while singing the film's best-known song.

Her story continues in Frozen II (2019), where she searches for the source of her powers. The franchise also includes Anna, the ice harvester Kristoff, and the snowman Olaf. Elsa is widely recognized for her platinum braid and her ice-blue gown.

Traits that give them away

  • Ice and snow magic. Her powers are central, so the superpowers question is a yes.
  • Queen of Arendelle. Royalty and leadership are major parts of her story.
  • Animated. She comes from an animated feature film.
  • A fantasy kingdom. Arendelle is a fairy-tale setting.
  • Family, not romance. Her story is about sisterhood rather than a love story, which is unusual for a Disney lead.

Easy to confuse with

Elsa is often confused with other Disney royalty such as Anna, Rapunzel, Cinderella, and Moana. The romance question is one of the best separators, since Elsa's story does not center on marriage, while those of several other princesses do. Characters with ice powers, such as Marvel's Iceman, also overlap on the power question.

Tips for playing as Elsa

Remember that Elsa and Anna are separate characters. If you want a harder round, think of Anna or Olaf and see how the answers change. For an easy round, answer as Elsa and emphasize the ice powers and the throne.
