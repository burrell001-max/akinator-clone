🍬 L (Death Note)

L is the brilliant, eccentric detective at the center of the anime and manga Death Note. He is known for his unkempt hair, his unusual way of sitting, and his love of sweets. He is the great intellectual rival of the story's main character.

Why the Genie sometimes needs a few extra questions

L is a medium pick because he shares a story with the main character, Light Yagami, and the Genie has to tell them apart. His lack of powers and his genius are the main clues.

Background on L (Death Note)

Death Note was written by Tsugumi Ohba and illustrated by Takeshi Obata, and ran in Weekly Shonen Jump from 2003 to 2006. An anime adaptation followed in 2006. The story begins when a student, Light Yagami, finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it. L is a mysterious detective who is recruited to catch the killer, known as Kira.

L hides his face and real name from the public, working through an assistant called Watari. The cat-and-mouse battle between him and Light is the heart of the series. L is known for his intellect and his willingness to take risks to win.

Traits that give them away

  • Genius detective. Intellect is his defining trait, so the mind-over-strength question is a yes.
  • A secret identity. He hides his face and name from the world.
  • No supernatural powers. Unlike Light, he does not use the notebook.
  • A rival. His battle with Light drives the story.
  • Anime and manga. He comes from an animated series.

Easy to confuse with

L is most often confused with Light Yagami, his rival, and with other detective characters such as Sherlock Holmes or Conan Edogawa. Light and L both attend the same story, but only Light uses the notebook, so the supernatural question helps. Near and Mello, L's successors, are other characters from the series.

Tips for playing as L (Death Note)

Think of L as a detective rather than a hero or villain. For a harder round, think of Near or Mello, who share many of L's traits. Be consistent about whether you are picturing the manga or the anime.
