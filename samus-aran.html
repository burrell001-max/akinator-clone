🚀 Samus Aran

Samus Aran is the armored bounty hunter at the center of Nintendo's Metroid series. Wearing a powerful Power Suit with an arm cannon, she explores hostile alien worlds and fights space pirates and dangerous creatures.

Why the Genie sometimes needs a few extra questions

Samus is a medium pick because she is less universally known than Mario or Link, and her suit hides her face. Questions about space, armor, and a solo protagonist narrow things down.

Background on Samus Aran

Metroid was released by Nintendo in 1986. Samus was a pioneering video game heroine, famous for the original game's ending, which revealed that the armored figure was a woman. She was raised partly by an alien race called the Chozo, who gave her the technology behind her suit.

In the series, she hunts the space pirates and the parasitic Metroid creatures, and frequently faces Ridley and Mother Brain. The series includes the side-scrolling Metroid games, the first-person Metroid Prime games that began in 2002, and Metroid Dread in 2021. Samus rarely speaks, so her story is told through action.

Traits that give them away

  • A bounty hunter. Her career makes the job question a yes.
  • Power Suit. Her abilities come mostly from her armor, so the powers question is mixed.
  • Space setting. The games take place on alien planets and space stations.
  • A solo protagonist. She is the title hero of her series.
  • Mostly silent. She rarely uses dialogue.

Easy to confuse with

Samus is often confused with other armored sci-fi heroes, such as Master Chief from Halo, Iron Man, or Ripley from Alien. Master Chief is the closest match, since both are helmeted space soldiers. The bounty hunter job, the solo-protagonist question, and the female lead help separate her.

Tips for playing as Samus Aran

Remember that Samus is human, so answer the alien question carefully; she is not from another species but lives in space. For an extra challenge, think of Ridley or Mother Brain from the same series.
