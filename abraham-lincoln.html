🎩 Abraham Lincoln

Abraham Lincoln was the 16th president of the United States, who led the country through the Civil War and worked to end slavery. He is regularly ranked among the greatest American presidents.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Lincoln is an easy pick because he is taught in nearly every American classroom and has several unmistakable traits: a president, the Civil War, and a tall figure in a top hat. Questions about leadership and a real person narrow things quickly.

Background on Abraham Lincoln

Lincoln was born in 1809 in a log cabin in Kentucky and grew up in Indiana and Illinois. He worked as a lawyer and served in the Illinois legislature and in Congress before winning the presidency in 1860. His election was followed by the secession of several Southern states and the start of the Civil War in 1861.

He issued the Emancipation Proclamation in 1863, delivered the Gettysburg Address the same year, and supported the 13th Amendment, which abolished slavery. He was assassinated by John Wilkes Booth at Ford's Theatre in April 1865, just days after the war's end. He was about six feet four inches tall, which made him one of the tallest presidents.

Traits that give them away

  • A national leader. The leadership question is a clear yes.
  • The Civil War. His presidency is defined by it.
  • A real person. He is a historical figure, so the fictional question is a no.
  • A lawyer by training. He worked as an attorney before politics.
  • An iconic look. His beard and tall hat are instantly recognizable.

Easy to confuse with

Lincoln is often confused with other American presidents such as George Washington, Franklin D. Roosevelt, and Thomas Jefferson. Washington led the Revolutionary War rather than the Civil War, and Roosevelt led during the Great Depression and World War II. The Civil War question is the strongest separator.
