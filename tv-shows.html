How to pick and answer for this category

TV characters change over time. A character in season one can be quite different by season six, so pick a point in the story before you start and answer for that version. This matters most for characters whose morals shift, such as Walter White and Saul Goodman, or whose role changes, such as Eleven.

Remember too that TV characters are often less universally known than movie stars. The Genie asks whether your character is known to almost everyone, and an honest No for a cult favorite like Omar Little is the right answer. For long-running figures such as The Doctor or Sherlock Holmes, remember that many actors have played them; answer for the version in your head.
