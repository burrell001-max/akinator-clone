How to pick and answer for this category

Anime characters raise one question that live-action characters rarely do: what counts as human? Goku is a Saiyan raised on Earth, Levi is an ordinary human with extraordinary skill, L is a human genius with no supernatural abilities, and Pikachu is not a person at all. Answer the Genie's "appears human" question according to how the character looks and behaves in the story, and use "Probably" when you are unsure.

Also decide whether you are thinking of the manga, the anime or the wider franchise. Dragon Ball spans several series and decades, and Pokemon appears in games, cartoons and films. The traits on each guide page describe the version that the Genie's data was written for.

Looking for a character that is not here yet? The roster is limited today, and expanding it carefully, with traits that are reviewed against the source material, is part of our plan.
