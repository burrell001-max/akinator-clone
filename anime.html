How to pick and answer for this category

Anime characters raise one question that live-action characters rarely do: what counts as human? Goku is a Saiyan raised on Earth, Levi is an ordinary human with extraordinary skill, L is a human genius with no supernatural abilities, and Pikachu is not a person at all. Answer the Genie's "appears human" question according to how the character looks and behaves in the story, and use "Probably" when you are unsure.

Also decide whether you are thinking of the manga, the anime or the wider franchise. Dragon Ball spans several series and decades, and Pokemon appears in games, cartoons and films. The traits on each guide page describe the version that the Genie's data was written for.

Looking for a character that is not here yet? The roster is limited today, and expanding it carefully, with traits that are reviewed against the source material, is part of our plan.

Series knowledge is the real advantage

With anime picks, the player who knows the series has a large edge over the player who only recognises the character. Dragon Ball characters change form, name and power level across decades of material; Death Note characters are defined by what they know and how they reason; Attack on Titan characters are defined by rank, unit and loyalty. None of that can be asked about directly, but it shapes the answers to the questions that can.

A useful habit is to fix the arc before you begin. A character from the first arc and the same character from a later arc may answer differently about rank, team membership or confidence, and mixing the two is the most common reason an anime round goes wrong.

What a hard rating means here

The difficulty label on each card describes how close that character sits to its nearest neighbour in the engine's trait data, not how obscure the character is. A hard rating means that one uncertain answer is enough to move the round onto a different character. It does not mean the character is rare, and it does not mean the round is unfair: everything the engine needs is something a viewer would know.

Frequently asked questions

How many anime characters are in the roster?

Four at present, listed above. The category is deliberately short: every card is a character the engine can actually guess.

Why is Pikachu listed under anime rather than video games?

Because he is best known through the animated series and films. He also appears in the games, so either category is a reasonable place to look.

Should I answer for the manga or the anime?

Whichever you know, as long as you stay with it. The two versions diverge in places, so mixing them produces answers that fit neither.

Will more anime characters be added?

Yes, when their traits can be recorded accurately. A character who changes heavily between arcs is harder to add than one with a stable role.
