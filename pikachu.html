⚡ Pikachu

Pikachu is the yellow, electric mouse-like creature that became the face of the Pokémon franchise. It is known for its red cheeks, its lightning-bolt tail, and its cry of its own name.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Pikachu is an easy pick because it is one of the most recognizable characters in the world, and it is non-human, animated, and able to use electricity. The 'is it human' and 'does it speak' questions narrow it down quickly.

Background on Pikachu

Pikachu debuted in the original Pokémon games for the Game Boy, released in Japan in 1996, and is number 25 in the Pokédex. It was designed by Atsuko Nishida and refined by Ken Sugimori. It became the franchise's mascot largely thanks to the anime, in which it is the loyal partner of a young trainer named Ash Ketchum.

Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon that can use attacks such as Thunderbolt. It evolves into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone, and it has a pre-evolved form called Pichu. It can only say variations of its name, so it communicates through tone and body language.

Traits that give them away

  • Not human. It is a creature, so the human question is a no.
  • Electric powers. Electric attacks make the powers question a yes.
  • Says only its name. It does not use human speech.
  • A loyal partner. In the anime it travels with Ash, so the companion question is a yes.
  • A global mascot. The fame question is an easy yes.

Easy to confuse with

Pikachu is often confused with other creature mascots such as Pichu, Raichu, Jigglypuff, and Sonic the Hedgehog. It is also sometimes mixed up with other Pokémon in the same series, like Eevee or Charmander. The electricity question is the quickest separator within the Pokémon franchise.
