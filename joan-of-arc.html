🛡️ Joan of Arc

Joan of Arc was a teenage peasant girl who led French armies during the Hundred Years' War and became a national heroine. She is also a Catholic saint.

Why the Genie sometimes needs a few extra questions

Joan is a medium pick because the Genie has to separate her from other famous historical figures, especially other rulers and generals. Questions about a woman, a military leader, and a medieval setting help.

Background on Joan of Arc

Joan was born around 1412 in Domremy, in northeastern France. In her teens she said she heard the voices of saints telling her to help the future King Charles VII drive the English from France. In 1429 she helped lift the siege of Orleans, a turning point in the war, and escorted Charles to Reims to be crowned.

She was captured by Burgundian forces in 1430 and handed over to the English, who put her on trial for heresy. She was executed in Rouen in 1431 at about nineteen years old. A later inquiry in 1456 cleared her name, and she was canonized in 1920.

Traits that give them away

  • A military leader. She led troops despite having no formal training.
  • A woman in a male-dominated age. Her gender is a defining feature of her story.
  • Religious visions. Her faith and claimed visions drove her mission.
  • A medieval setting. She lived in the early fifteenth century.
  • A real person. She is a historical figure, though many portrayals are dramatized.

Easy to confuse with

Joan is often mixed up with other warrior women such as Boudica or Mulan, and with other medieval figures such as Richard the Lionheart or William Wallace. Mulan is fictional, which the first question settles right away. Boudica was an ancient British queen, far earlier than Joan.

Tips for playing as Joan of Arc

Joan is a good pick if you want a real person who is easy to describe. If you want a harder round, think of one of the people around her, such as Charles VII. Answer questions about leadership carefully: she led armies but was never a ruler.
