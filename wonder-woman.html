By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Why the round is short

Superhuman, heroic, royal, armed and part of a team: those answers clear most of the roster quickly and leave a small group of superheroes.

The partial is what keeps her alive next to Thor. Both are stored as only partly human, so a round cannot discard either on the human question alone.

Who she is

An Amazon princess raised on Themyscira, an island hidden from the world. In the films she leaves during the First World War; in the comics she arrives in a world that treats her as hero or threat depending on the decade.

Her defining object is the lasso, which forces the truth out of whoever holds it, and her defining conflict is between the rules of home and the mess outside it.

Where players get tripped up

The human question. A flat no is defensible, but the engine stores a partial, and a no pushes the round towards aliens and androids.

The costume question is a partial too. Her armour is iconic but it is not a disguise, and she has no secret identity in the usual sense.

Answer those differences deliberately and the round resolves; answer by habit and the guess is a coin toss between the two.

A psychologist's invention

William Moulton Marston helped develop the lie detector, and the lasso of truth is the fictional version of that interest. The character was designed to win an argument about how stories should portray strength, which is why her early comics read so differently from the superheroes around her.

The 2017 film is the version most players picture now, and Lynda Carter's television version is the one their parents picture. The stored answers are the same for both.
