⚡ Harry Potter

Harry Potter is the young wizard at the center of J.K. Rowling's seven-book series, which became one of the best-selling book series of all time and a blockbuster film franchise. His lightning-bolt scar, round glasses, and wand are recognizable even to people who have never opened a book.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Harry sits in the Genie's easy tier because he combines several highly distinctive traits: he is a wizard, he attends a magical school, he has a famous trio of friends, and he faces one defined arch-enemy. A handful of yes/no questions about magic, school, and a fantasy setting is usually enough to point to him.

Background on Harry Potter

Harry Potter is the title character of the seven-book series by J.K. Rowling, which began with the first novel in 1997 and was adapted into eight blockbuster films. He is an orphan raised by his unpleasant aunt and uncle who learns on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard and has been accepted to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

Over the series he makes close friends in Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, discovers the truth about the lightning-bolt scar on his forehead, and faces the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who killed his parents. Because the story began in books and became a film franchise, Harry works as both a 'book' and a 'movie' character, which is something to keep in mind when answering.

Traits that give them away

  • Magic. He is a wizard, so powers are a clear yes.
  • A school setting. Much of the story takes place at Hogwarts, so the school question is a strong signal.
  • Famous friends. Ron and Hermione make the companion question a yes.
  • A defined nemesis. Voldemort is the arch-enemy at the center of the series.
  • Fantasy world. The wizarding world sits alongside the ordinary one, so the fantasy setting question is a yes.

A sample round

  5. Does your character attend a school or academy as part of their story? Yes
  6. Is your character's story set in a fantasy or mythical world? Yes
  7. Does your character have a famous sidekick or loyal companion? Yes
  8. Does your character have a nemesis or arch-enemy? Yes

Easy to confuse with

The character most often confused with Harry is Hermione Granger, who shares the setting and the school. Others in the same neighborhood include Albus Dumbledore (the elderly mentor), Ron Weasley, and Percy Jackson from a different series with a similar young-hero-at-a-magical-school shape. Asking whether the character is a mentor figure, and whether they are the title protagonist, separates Harry from the rest of his circle quickly.

Tips for playing as Harry Potter

Harry is a good character for beginners because his traits are so well known. To make it harder, think of a lesser-known student or teacher from the same books and notice how many of the same answers still apply. For a clean round, choose whether you are thinking of the book character or the film version and answer consistently.
