GLaDOS: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

GLaDOS answers yes to powers, science, villainy, leadership, an enemy and mind-over-strength, and no to being human. That combination is close enough to Cortana that the two are each other's nearest match, and the round turns on the villain question.

Quick facts

First appearance
Portal (2007)
Created by
Valve
Role
Overseer of the Aperture Science testing facility
Known for
Passive aggression, testing and cake
Powers
Full control of the facility; no physical body

Why the round is short

Science and villainy together are rare in this roster, and adding an enemy and leadership narrows it to a couple of names. Most rounds reach her quickly.

The split from Cortana is villainy. Cortana is stored as heroic, GLaDOS is stored as a villain, and that single answer resolves what the rest of the sheet leaves ambiguous.

Who she is

The artificial intelligence running Aperture Science, who puts test subjects through lethal puzzles and comments on their performance. She is the antagonist of Portal and the co-lead of Portal 2.

She has no body in the first game and a body in the second, which is why the engine stores no costume answer but does store a transformation.

Traits the engine stores

  • Fictional: Yes.
  • Powers: Yes.
  • Hero: No.
  • Human or appears human: No.
  • Science or tech: Yes.
  • Villain: Yes.
  • Known to almost everyone: Partly.
  • Royalty or leadership: Yes.
  • Nemesis: Yes.
  • Mind over strength: Yes.
  • Speaks: Yes.
  • Real name known: Yes.

Where players get tripped up

The human question. She is voiced by a human and behaves like a person, so yes feels natural, but she is an AI and the engine stores a no.

The job question is a no. Running a facility is a function rather than employment, and a yes points at characters like Dumbledore.

Why the Genie might guess Cortana instead

Closest match on the sheet: Cortana, at an average distance of 0.16 across 28 questions.

They differ on 6 questions: hero no against yes; villain yes against no; team or organisation no against partly; royalty or leadership yes against no; nemesis yes against partly; major transformation no against partly. Answer those differences deliberately and the round resolves; answer by habit and the guess is a coin toss between the two.

An antagonist with a voice

Ellen McLain voiced GLaDOS and also sang the end-credit song, which is why the character's menace and the game's humour are the same instrument. The passive aggression is scripted; the timing is performance.

Portal 2 gives her a body, a backstory and a reluctant partnership with the player, and the co-op campaign pairs her with a second robot who is not allowed to speak. The engine records the transformation and the leadership, which is more than the first game alone would justify.
