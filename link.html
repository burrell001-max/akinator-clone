🗡️ Link

Link is the recurring hero of a long-running fantasy action-adventure franchise, typically tasked with rescuing a princess and defeating an ancient evil across a large open world. Despite rarely speaking in most games, he's one of gaming's most enduring protagonists.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

A fantasy adventure setting, a signature green tunic, and a silent-protagonist trait that's unusual among major game characters make Link easy for the Genie to isolate quickly, typically after just a couple of questions about genre and setting.

Background on Link

Link is the hero of The Legend of Zelda, the Nintendo franchise created by Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka that began in 1986. A common point of confusion is the title: the series is named after Princess Zelda, but the player controls Link, a young adventurer from the kingdom of Hyrule.

In most games, Link wields the Master Sword, collects pieces of the Triforce, and fights the villain Ganon (also known as Ganondorf) to save Hyrule and Zelda. Link is usually a silent protagonist who communicates through gestures and expressions rather than dialogue, and many different versions of Link appear across games, from Ocarina of Time to Breath of the Wild, released in 2017.

Traits that give them away

  • Silent protagonist. He rarely speaks, which makes the human-speech question tricky.
  • Sword and shield. The Master Sword and Hylian Shield are iconic.
  • Fantasy world. Hyrule is a fantasy kingdom with magic, monsters, and ancient ruins.
  • A recurring nemesis. Ganon is the main antagonist of the series.
  • Many incarnations. Each game features a different Link, which can make answers inconsistent.

Easy to confuse with

The most common confusion is with Zelda herself, because of the series title. Link is also confused with Peter Pan (due to the green tunic), with Cloud Strife from Final Fantasy VII (a sword-wielding game hero), and with Mario as another Nintendo icon. Questions about the sword, the fantasy setting, and the silent protagonist are the most useful.

Tips for playing as Link

Because Link is a different person in many games, decide on one version before you start. For a harder round, choose Zelda or Ganondorf from the same series and notice how the answers change.
