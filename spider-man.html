🕷️ Spider-Man

Spider-Man is Marvel's friendly neighborhood hero: a teenager from Queens, New York, who gains spider-like abilities and tries to balance saving the city with homework, rent, and family. He is one of the most widely adapted superheroes ever, with comics, cartoons, video games, and many live-action films.

Why the Genie usually gets this one

Spider-Man is an easy pick for the Genie because several of his traits appear together: superpowers, a mask, a secret identity, and a school-age hero. A few questions about powers, masks, and school are usually enough to separate him from most other heroes.

Background on Spider-Man

Spider-Man was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko and first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962. Peter Parker is a shy high-school student who is bitten by a radioactive spider and gains superhuman strength, agility, and a sixth sense for danger. After his Uncle Ben is killed, he commits to using his abilities responsibly.

Over the decades Peter has been joined by other people who carry the name, most notably Miles Morales. On screen, the role has been played by Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield, and Tom Holland, and animated versions have won major awards. His allies include Aunt May and Mary Jane Watson, and his enemies include the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and Venom.

Traits that give them away

  • Spider powers. Wall-crawling, super strength, and a danger sense make the powers question an easy yes.
  • Mask and costume. The red-and-blue suit and mask are central to his identity.
  • Secret identity. Most stories depend on nobody knowing that Peter Parker is Spider-Man.
  • School setting. Many versions put him in high school, so the school question is usually a yes.
  • A famous rogues' gallery. Enemies like the Green Goblin make the nemesis question a clear yes.

Easy to confuse with

Spider-Man is most often confused with other masked heroes such as Batman, Daredevil, and Deadpool. Batman has no powers. Deadpool is a mercenary who is far more violent and breaks the fourth wall. Miles Morales is a different Spider-Man with a different background, so decide which one you mean. The school question and the powers question separate Spider-Man from most of the others.

Tips for playing as Spider-Man

Spider-Man has many versions, so pick one and stay consistent. If you want to stump the Genie, think of Miles Morales, Gwen Stacy, or Venom and notice how many answers change. For a quick round, answer as the classic Peter Parker.
