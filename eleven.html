🔦 Eleven

Eleven is a central character in Stranger Things, the Netflix series set in 1980s Indiana. She is a young girl with telekinetic powers who escapes a secret government laboratory and befriends a group of local kids.

Why the Genie sometimes needs a few extra questions

Eleven takes a little more work than the biggest names because the show is an ensemble. Several characters share the same setting, friends, and enemies, so the Genie leans on her powers and her role in the group.

Background on Eleven

Stranger Things was created by the Duffer Brothers and premiered on Netflix in 2016. Eleven, played by Millie Bobby Brown, is called by a number tattooed on her wrist, since she was raised in the Hawkins National Laboratory by a scientist she calls Papa. She can move objects with her mind, sense things at a distance, and enter a dark void.

After she escapes, she meets Mike, Dustin, and Lucas, and is eventually taken in by the town's police chief, Jim Hopper. Her powers are tied to the show's central threat, the Upside Down, a dark mirror of the town. Over the seasons she grows from a silent, frightened child into a determined young woman.

Traits that give them away

  • Telekinesis. Her abilities make the superpowers question a clear yes.
  • A group of friends. She is part of a close-knit team.
  • Sparse speech. She speaks in short sentences, especially in early seasons.
  • An 1980s setting. The show is set in a small American town in the 1980s.
  • Growth over time. She changes a lot between seasons, so the transformation question is a yes.

Easy to confuse with

Eleven is commonly confused with other young heroes with powers, such as Wednesday Addams or Matilda. Within the show, the other kids, such as Max, Will, and Mike, share a lot of traits except the telekinesis. Characters from other science-fiction series, such as Jean Grey from the X-Men, also overlap on the power question.

Tips for playing as Eleven

Decide which season you are picturing, since Eleven changes substantially across the show. For a harder round, think of one of the other kids, or of Hopper, and see how the answers shift.
