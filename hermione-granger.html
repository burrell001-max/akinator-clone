Hermione Granger: A Character Guide for MindGenie AI

By Kent B Williams · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Hermione is the clearest test of the engine's weak spot. She shares nearly every stored answer with Harry Potter, so the round cannot separate them on the obvious questions and has to reach the couple where they differ. Expect a long round, and expect the split to come down to work and billing.

Quick facts

Role
Witch, student, later a Ministry official
Known for

Powers
Wand magic, learned rather than inherited

Why the round is slow

The engine keeps the character whose stored answers sit closest to yours. Hermione and Harry agree on fiction, powers, heroism, humanity, school, fantasy, team, companion, enemy and fame, which is most of the sheet.

Two answers differ. Hermione has a workplace and Harry does not, and Harry is the sole title-billed protagonist while she is not. If a round skips both, the two are effectively tied and the guess comes down to roster order.

Who she is

A Muggle-born Gryffindor who becomes friends with Harry and Ron after a troll gets into the bathroom. She is the planner: the one who has read the book, remembers the spell and spots the detail.

Her arc is about being taken seriously in a world that treats her background as a curiosity, and later material puts her in the Ministry of Magic.

Where players get tripped up

The protagonist question. She is central to the story but not the title-billed lead, and the engine stores a no.

The job question cuts the other way. Players think of her as a student and answer no, which erases the only reliable difference between her and Harry.

Why the Genie might guess Harry Potter instead

Closest match on the sheet: Harry Potter, at an average distance of 0.12 across 28 questions.

They differ on 5 questions: science or tech yes against no; signature weapon partly against yes; mind over strength yes against partly; major transformation partly against yes; sole protagonist no against yes. Answer those differences deliberately and the round resolves; answer by habit and the guess is a coin toss between the two.

Books, films and the stage play

The films cut most of Hermione's political subplot, which is why players who only know the films describe her as the clever one and stop there. The books give her a campaign against house-elf slavery and a stubbornness that the films soften.

The stage play adds a different adult version of her, and the engine does not distinguish between them. If a round feels inconsistent, the version in the player's head is usually the reason.
