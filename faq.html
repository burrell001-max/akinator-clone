Frequently Asked Questions

Is MindGenie AI free to play?

Yes. The game is completely free, with no sign-up, download, or in-game purchases required.

How accurate is MindGenie AI?

Accuracy depends on how well-known the character is and how consistently you answer. Popular characters are usually guessed correctly within 10–20 questions, while obscure or very similar characters can trip up the Genie.

How many questions does the Genie ask?

Usually 10–20 questions, depending on your answers.

Can I play MindGenie AI on my phone?

Yes. The game runs entirely in the browser and is fully playable on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Why does the game say it's "waking up" sometimes?

Our guessing engine runs on a free-tier server that goes to sleep when it isn't being used. The first game of the day can take up to 30 seconds to "wake up" — after that, rounds load quickly.

What kinds of characters can I think of?

Almost anything — fictional characters from movies, TV, books, and games, as well as well-known historical figures or celebrities. The wider known the character, the more likely the Genie will guess correctly.

Does the Genie always guess correctly?

No — it's a fun guessing model, not a perfect mind reader. Obscure or very similar characters can trip it up, which is part of the fun. See our list of characters that stump the Genie if you want a challenge.

Does MindGenie AI store who I'm thinking of?

We don't ask for or store any personal information to play. See our Privacy Policy for full details on cookies and analytics used on this site.

Can I play more than once?

Yes — there's no limit. Once a round ends, click "Challenge Me Again" to start a new one right away.

Is MindGenie AI like Akinator?

Yes — it's a free browser-based character guessing game in the same spirit as Akinator: think of a character, answer yes-or-no style questions, and see if the Genie can guess who it is. You can read more about where this format comes from in our history of 20 Questions games post.

Does MindGenie AI use artificial intelligence?

Yes — the Genie narrows down possible characters based on your answers, refining its questions and guesses as more people play. Curious how? Read how AI guessing games work.

Can MindGenie AI guess movie and anime characters?

Absolutely. Movies and anime are two of the most popular categories, alongside TV, superheroes, history, video games, and celebrities.
